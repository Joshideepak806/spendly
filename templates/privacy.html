Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when creating an account, along with the expense records you add — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We also collect basic usage information, such as login timestamps, to help keep your account secure.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core functionality: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third-party services. Where third-party infrastructure (such as hosting or email delivery) is used to operate the service, those providers only process data as needed to support Spendly and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
